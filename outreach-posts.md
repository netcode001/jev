### 标题

Jev dropped its waitlist and gives everyone $5 in free credit — here's what I learned tracking its real costs since launch

### 正文

Quick context for anyone who missed launch week: Jev is TypeSafe's "System One" model — it doesn't generate text at all. It returns typed decisions (Choice / Score / Noul) with a confidence value, designed to be consumed by code, not humans.

Since Sep 20 it's fully open: no waitlist, and new accounts at console.typesafe.ai get **$5 in free credit** — which at the list price ($0.042/1M input tokens, output unmetered) is about 120 million tokens.

I've been tracking everything Jev since launch day at https://jev-ai.live/ (news, pricing snapshots, ecosystem). Things I learned along the way:

**1. The billing shape matters more than the multiple.** The "444x cheaper" headline compares a model that bills input-only against models that also bill every output token. Part of the gap is the billing shape, not pure discount. Still very real for high-volume classification — just not "a cheaper ChatGPT."

**2. The benchmarks are self-reported.** 67.8% on TypeSafe's own 4-workflow eval, with consensus labels generated by two frontier models rather than ground truth. No independent reproduction yet. Every benchmark number on my site is labeled accordingly.

**3. Watch out for the OpenRouter misinformation.** Launch-week posts claimed Jev was listed on OpenRouter. Its public models API returns no TypeSafe entries. The reliable paths: console.typesafe.ai (direct, now open), Vercel AI Gateway (typesafe-ai/jev), Cloudflare Workers AI (typesafe/jev — note 32K context, not 64K).

**4. The ecosystem is already real.** QuantDinger (11.8k stars) uses Jev as the decision layer in an AI trading OS; kev (1k stars) is a self-hostable Jev-like model family on Qwen3.5; jev-review (410 stars) does staged code review. One community demo classified 1,018 research papers for $0.08 total at 256ms median latency.

**5. The mental model that clicks:** LLM for prose, Jev for the hundreds of small decisions around it. "Is this ticket billing or technical?" is a Jev question. "Write the reply" is not.
